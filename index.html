MP3 audio

MP3 reads the whole text from the beginning and saves it as an MP3 file.

MP3 export works in Chrome and Edge on a computer. When asked, choose This tab and turn on Share tab audio.

If the file is silent, try again, choose Entire screen and turn on Share system audio.
